================================================================================
SHIVAAYAHA SILK SAREES — RECORD PAYMENT FEATURE (USES & USER GUIDE)
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS "RECORD PAYMENT"?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Record Payment" feature in Shivaayaha Silk Sarees is your digital manual 
cash/voucher book (Khata / Paid Voucher). Because this application is built 
specifically for 100% manual bookkeeping without payment gateways, "Record Payment" 
is how you record every single rupee paid out to weavers, saree artisans, and 
wholesale yarn/fabric suppliers.


2. KEY USES & PRACTICAL EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Cash Paid on Delivery (Hand-to-Hand Cash):
   - When a weaver delivers silk sarees to your shop and you pay cash on the 
     spot, record it as "Cash" payment. The weaver's pending due drops immediately.

B. Online UPI Payments (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm):
   - When you transfer money through your mobile phone UPI app, choose "Manual UPI"
     and copy/type the 12-digit UTR reference number (e.g. 423985109283). 
     This prevents disputes later on.

C. Paying Off a Specific Bill / Saree Lot:
   - When you want to settle a particular invoice or delivery lot, select the bill 
     under "Link To Specific Bill / Lot". The bill automatically updates from 
     UNPAID -> PARTIAL -> PAID.

D. General Lump-Sum / Advance Payments:
   - When making a partial round-figure payment (e.g. Rs. 50,000) against a large 
     running balance, select "General Account Balance". It reduces the total due 
     across the weaver's account.

E. Cheques and Bank Transfers:
   - Record post-dated or cleared cheques with the Cheque number (e.g. Chq #000412),
     or NEFT/IMPS transfers for easy matching with bank statements.

F. Linking Physical Paper Slips & Shop Diary Vouchers:
   - If you maintain physical paper slips or a voucher book at your counter, 
     type the voucher number and location in the "Paper Slip Memo" field.


3. STEP-BY-STEP HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Click the "+ Record Payment" button on the Dashboard or Weavers page.
Step 2: Select the Weaver / Wholesale Party.
        (The box will show their Current Pending Due).
Step 3: (Optional) Select a specific Bill / Lot if paying towards a single bill.
Step 4: Enter the Payment Amount in Rupees (Rs.) and select the Date.
Step 5: Select Payment Mode: Cash, Manual UPI, Cheque, or IMPS/NEFT.
Step 6: Enter the UTR / Cheque / Voucher Number in the Reference field.
Step 7: (Optional) Add any notes in "Paper Slip Memo".
Step 8: Click "Post Manual Voucher (Paid)".


4. AUTOMATIC SYSTEM UPDATES WHEN YOU RECORD PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The weaver's "Current Pending Due" decreases immediately.
2. If linked to an invoice, the bill's balance due updates and marks as PAID once 
   fully settled.
3. Dashboard metrics instantly reflect the new "Total Paid" and reduced 
   "Total Outstanding Due".
4. The payment is permanently logged in the Transactions history and party 
   statement, ready for one-click PDF download or WhatsApp sharing.
